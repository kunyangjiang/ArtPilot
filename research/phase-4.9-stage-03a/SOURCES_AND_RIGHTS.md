Checked 2026-10-27. Met data from object pages read in a browser (Overview, Signatures/Inscriptions and Provenance
tabs; the Provenance radio needs a real click) and the collection API (`/v1/objects/{id}`; search via
`/v1.1/search`). Cleveland data from its open-access API (`openaccess-api.clevelandart.org`). British Museum, Freer
(asia.si.edu) and UNESCO pages read in the built-in browser. Wikimedia Commons licence data from the Commons API
(`imageinfo` + `extmetadata`, and the page wikitext where the template mattered); files downloaded from the original
upload URL. Every image was resized to at most 1600 px on its long side and recompressed; crops are noted. Nothing
was brightened, recoloured or annotated in the file itself (rings, boxes and marks are drawn by the page).
CC BY-SA images: the resized files are offered under the same licence.

"PD-Art" below means: Commons marks the file public domain as a faithful photographic reproduction of a
two-dimensional work whose author died long ago (the US rule; other countries differ). This follows the precedent of
the Fan Kuan record (Phase 4.0).

  compartment (the excavation report was not read).
- **Museum display:** the reconstruction of fan, armrest, screen and mat in the gallery is flagged as an exhibition
  arrangement (reported in search results; not a claim about the tomb).
