## Infrastructure

- New components (narrow, no new infrastructure):
  - `CatenaryModel.astro` — one hanging chain; choose where bags of weight hang; turn it upside down to see the
    compression arch; optional semicircle overlay. The curve is computed (funicular polygon ∝ bending-moment diagram
    of a simply supported beam under the same loads; self-weight as equal point loads, so the unloaded case is a
    parabola, stated in the schematic label). Reduced motion: no flip animation. Stacks below 760 px.

## Story 10 — Zhao Mengfu

**Engine.** Political and personal identity across the Song–Yuan transition, framed by the 1319 letter written after
escorting his wife's coffin “三千里” home; the story goes back to his lineage and returns to the letter.

**Primary documents examined.**
- NPM open data, 元赵孟頫赵氏一门法书 册: letters to Zhongfeng Mingben, ids 76 (六月十二日, “得旨南还，何图病妻道卒”,
  30.7 × 62.7 cm), 77 (六月廿八日, “整卅年”), 78 (“三十年夫妇……今既将半载”), 80 (四月十二日, “体疲眼暗”, “先妻”);
  Guan Daosheng's own letter, id 84 (“手书般若经……救荐亡儿女”, “但得到家，只就家庭修设”). Full transcriptions read.
- NPM open data, 元人集锦 卷 · 管道昇烟雨丛竹 (id 17466): signature “至大元年……为楚国夫人作于碧浪湖舟中”; Qianlong's poem.
- The Met, *Twin Pines, Level Distance* (1973.120.5): description; artist's title and long inscription; colophons by
  Yang Zai and Tong Xuan (“然文敏以故宋宗室之裔，委质元主……”), full text from the inscriptions tab.
- The Met, *Grooms and Horses* (1988.135): description (compass and square; Bole; groom “may be a self-portrait”);
  1296 inscription; 1359 inscriptions by Zhao Yong and Zhao Lin; colophons by Hu Cheng (1405) and Shen Danian (1403).
- 《元史》卷172, 赵孟頫传 and 程钜夫传 (Wikisource): lineage, 1286 recruitment, the paper-money debate, Sangha, Che-li,
  “汝赵太祖孙耶”, Jinan, Renzong, replacing Cheng Jufu, death 1322.
- Secondary: the 秀石疏林图 poem via The Paper (not checked against the Palace Museum catalogue; variant noted).

**Images.** NPM (CC BY 4.0, credit line “品名。国立故宫博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw”): the 1319 letter
(K2B000253N000000004PAA, table and colour card cropped away); Guan's bamboo (K2A001108N000000000PAZ, cropped to the
painting). The Met (Public Domain, Open Access CC0): *Twin Pines* (full, resized to 720 px high; two crops at original
resolution: pines and inscription); *Grooms and Horses* (the Met's own image of Zhao's section, recompressed).
*Autumn Colours on the Qiao and Hua Mountains* (course 29) is referenced in one line only.

**Uncertainties stated in the text.** Marriage year (1286 or 1289 in secondary sources; the letters' “三十年”); the
two biographies' years for the recruitment (1286/1287); where Guan died (not in the NPM data); whether the groom is a
self-portrait; whether the pines carry a political meaning; why Zhao served the Yuan (InferenceChain: unknowable).

## Story 11 — Artemisia Gentileschi

**Engine.** Documents versus paintings: a trial record, a marriage contract, an academy matriculation, love letters,
business letters, a royal inventory — set against five paintings. The 1612 trial is told from the record only, with
no description of the assault and no invented speech; the Judith is explicitly not read as a confession.

**Sources.** National Gallery, London: NG6671 page and 2024 catalogue entry (technical findings: Uffizi X-ray, tracing
match, transfer method, pentimenti; marriage contract 11 Aug 1612, wedding 29 Nov 1612; Florence addresses; 19 July
1616 matriculation; 14 May 1612 *sibille*; 13 Nov 1649 letter on models); “Artemisia in her own words” (trial and Ruffo
quotations, English, translated here). The Met, 69.281 catalogue entry (Christiansen 2014): rape 1611, “married off”,
self-declared illiterate, itinerary, Veronese source, X-rayed boy and dog, Locker's reading, dating debate, Voss 1924.
Royal Collection RCIN 405551 (Ripa, two-mirror hypothesis, finger change, Charles I inventory 1649, sale 1651).
Uffizi (c. 1620, 146.5 × 108, Cosimo II, Galileo, refused display). Britannica (family, trial cause and outcome, five
children, five Judiths, London). Susanna date/attribution debate via the Wikipedia summary of Garrard, Treves,
Christiansen & Mann (marked as a summary in the sources band).

**Images.** The Met *Esther* (CC0). Wikimedia Commons PD-Art faithful reproductions of public-domain paintings:
Uffizi *Judith* (Google Art Project, levels adjusted by the uploader — noted), Pommersfelden *Susanna* (Web Gallery of
Art), NG *Saint Catherine* (NG image), Royal Collection *La Pittura*. Paintings are 17th-century, public domain.

**Uncertainties stated.** The Susanna's last digit (1610 / 1616 / 1619) and Orazio's share; “first” vs “one of the
first” women in the Accademia; London dates (1634 / 1638 / 1639); Esther's date; how many models; death after 1654.

## Story 12 — Claude Monet

**Engine.** A real place, made and remade: back and forth between the 1927 Orangerie opening and the 1893 marsh;
the bridge of 1900 against the bridge of c. 1920–22.

**Sources.** AIC records 1933.441, 1933.1157, 1956.1202, 1985.1103, 2013.986, 1922.4465 (descriptions, inscriptions,
dimensions). The Met 29.100.113 (text only: 1893 purchase, “for the pleasure of the eye and also for motifs to paint”,
eighteen views/twelve completed in 1899; audio guide on the diverted river). MoMA 79254 / 242.1956 (date, size).
Musée de l'Orangerie, “History of the Water Lilies cycle” (1897, 1909, 1914, 1916, 12 Nov 1918, 1920, 12 Apr 1922,
Clemenceau's words, 1927, rooms, 1944, 1960s, 2006) and its teaching dossier (2 × 12.75 m). Britannica (birth,
Le Havre, Boudin, Gleyre, Japanese prints before 1872, Camille and Alice, 1883). Kopplin, *Hektoen International*
(1912 diagnosis, Coutela 1922, January 1923 surgery, aftermath, Clemenceau 1919, 1925); Marmor 2006 cited.

**Images.** AIC Open Access (CC0): five works at 1686 px. MoMA *Japanese Footbridge* via Wikimedia Commons (PD-Art).
The Met no longer allows downloads of its Monet images; none is used. Course 60/61 works (Rouen, AIC 1982.825) are
referenced in one line only.

**Uncertainties stated.** When the house was bought (1883 vs 1890); how much of the late change is the cataract
(Callout: not every late painting is a symptom); where the large canvases were painted (marked as this site's
inference from size).

## Story 13 — Antoni Gaudí

**Engine.** Two timelines: Gaudí designing with hanging models (1852–1926) and others rebuilding from fragments
(1936–2026). Gaudí's work, work built later to his plans, and later artists' own work are told apart (SortGame).

**Construction status.** Verified on 29 October 2026 at https://sagradafamilia.org/en/history-of-the-temple: Tower of
Jesus Christ exterior completed 20 February 2026 (upper arm of the cross), blessed and first lit 10 June 2026;
construction continues; no completion date for the whole basilica is given. The record and story say exactly this.

**Sources.** Sagrada Família Foundation: history timeline; Gaudí biography and method; polyfunicular model news
(original lost, 1983 reproduction; inclined branching pillars and hyperbolic paraboloids); blog “How much of the
Sagrada Família did Gaudí leave behind” (7 June 1926 tram; what was designed, modelled or only sketched); blog on the
columns (double-twist geometry, height rule, stones chosen later, concrete core). Colònia Güell crypt site (cords and
lead-shot bags; 1908–1914; only the crypt; materials). UNESCO No. 320 (synthesis; authenticity of the part built by
Gaudí; crypt columns not receiving their designed load).

**Images, two layers.** Gaudí died 1926: his designs are public domain (Spain: 80 years p.m.a., to the end of 2006;
US: pre-1990 architecture unprotected). Photographs: Baldomer Gili Roig, 1905 (photographer died 1926; public domain);
CC BY-SA 2.0 (Andrew Moore, Nativity façade; matilde.m.s, crypt); CC BY-SA 4.0 (Mikani, Casa Milà attic; Canaan,
2026 cross). The Nativity façade carries some later sculpture and the 2026 cross is a current team's work: both stand
permanently on public streets, covered by Spain's panorama exception (TRLPI art. 35.2). Not used: the 1983 hanging
model (museum interior; replaced by this site's `CatenaryModel`), the Passion façade (Subirachs, d. 2014), interiors
completed after 1986.

**Uncertainties stated.** How many model fragments were recovered (Callout open); dates of individual Nativity
sculptures not assigned; the authorship question left open, with UNESCO's wording given.

## Story 14 — Nek Chand

**Engine.** A secret environment: how it was made, found and kept; conflicting chronology shown, not resolved
(`SourceDates`). No police plot or discovery scene is invented; who found it and when is given only as the sources do.

**Sources.** Nek Chand Foundation: About (1951, 1958, early 1960s / c. 1965, 1975, 24 Jan 1976, 1989, materials,
25 / 40 acres, 3,000 / 5,000 visitors, Phase II ~5,000 figures, honours, 1997) and Background (Creator-Director with
fifty labourers; support declining from 1988; the 1990 road plan and bulldozers; court case; 1996 vandalism while
he toured the US — attributed to the Foundation). JMKAC: artist page and Rock Garden page (Berian Kala; walking to New
Delhi; 1951; villages razed; 1965; 25 acres; 150+ works from 1996 over eight years / 1999). Britannica (15 Dec 1924;
1955; 1958; eighteen years; ~20 villages; 1965; no-building zone; 1972, 12 acres; fifty employees; ~30 acres;
armature and surfaces; 1980/1983/1984; Washington garden dismantled 2004; 12 June 2015). American Folk Art Museum,
*Bangle Man*, 1984, 2004.25.23 (materials, 43 × 19 × 12 in, provenance from the Washington Fantasy Garden; “Copyright
for this work is under review”).

**Rights.** Nek Chand died 2015; his works are in copyright (India: 60 years p.m.a.). India's s.52(1)(t) would allow
photographs of sculptures permanently in public places, but not necessarily for readers elsewhere, and AFAM marks the
work's copyright as under review. No photograph of the garden or any figure is used. The card is typographic; the
record carries the official AFAM page; the only image is this site's diagram (armature → concrete → shards), drawn
from Britannica's and AFAM's descriptions.

**Card choice.** The card is *Bangle Man* rather than the garden itself: the site contract gives buildings no artist,
and a text-only card needs artist, medium and an official page. *Bangle Man* came from Chand's Washington garden,
dismantled in 2004, and fits the story's survival thread.

**Uncertainties stated.** Arrival 1951 / 1955; start early 1960s / 1965; discovery 1972 / 1975; land described three
ways; size 12 → 25 / 30 / 40 acres; visitors; 1989 vs post-1990 court case; JMKAC's 1996 vs 1999 gift; whether he
worked alone; his intentions (InferenceChain: unknowable).
